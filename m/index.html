About

Songs with nowhere to hide.

Gaz Kelly is an independent singer-songwriter and live performer based in Manchester. Pairing acoustic guitar with exposed, expressive vocals, he creates music that feels immediate, personal and deliberately unfiltered.

Rather than burying the story beneath heavy production, Gaz places the words and performance at the centre of each song. Releases including 555, Marijuana Blues and Photogenic introduced that stripped-back approach, while his debut album, Some Stories Don't Have Happy Endings, takes it into a fuller body of work.
